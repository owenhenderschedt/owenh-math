\textbf{Problem.}

Yoshi is considering a loan of \$400{,}000 to purchase a home. Two level-payment mortgage options with monthly payments are available:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Loan A (30-year): nominal annual rate $6.5\%$ compounded monthly.
    \item Loan B (50-year): nominal annual rate $6.875\%$ compounded monthly.
\end{itemize}

Calculate the difference in total interest paid over the full term of the loans.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\Alph*.]
    \item $159{,}700$
    \item $345{,}000$
    \item $511{,}000$
    \item $673{,}000$
    \item $960{,}000$
\end{enumerate}
