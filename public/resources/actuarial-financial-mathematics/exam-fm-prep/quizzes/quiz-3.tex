\noindent\textbf{Question 1.}  



A borrower takes out a loan of \$5{,}620 to be repaid with 10 annual payments made at the end of each year where the first payment is $P$. The annual effective interest rate is $6\%$.

The first five payments increase by $3\%$ each year and after the fifth payment, the payments decrease by $2\%$ each year for the next five years.

Find the value of $P$.

\medskip

\begin{center}

\textbf{(A)} $700$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(B)} $720$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(C)} $740$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(D)} $780$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(E)} $800$

\end{center}

\textbf{Question 2.}

At an annual effective interest rate of $5\%$, Fred and Carl have payment arrangements with the same present value.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Fred receives a perpetuity due with annual payments of $P$.
    
    \item Carl receives an increasing annuity with annual end-of-year payments for 10 years. The payment at time $t$ is
    \[
    P + 65(t-1), \qquad t = 1,2,\dots,10.
    \]
\end{itemize}

Find $P$.



\medskip

\begin{center}

\textbf{(A)} $120$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(B)} $155$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(C)} $185$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(D)} $210$
\hspace{1cm}
\textbf{(E)} $265$

\end{center}
