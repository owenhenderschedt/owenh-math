\noindent\textbf{Question 1.}  

Logo purchases an annuity that pays monthly amounts as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Payments of $X$ at times $t = 18, 19, \ldots, 35$
    \item Payments of $X + 500(t - 35)$ at times $t = 36, 37, \ldots, 71$
\end{itemize}

where $t$ is measured in months.

The annual effective interest rate is $10\%$.

The present value of all payments is $370{,}763.65$.

Calculate $X$.
