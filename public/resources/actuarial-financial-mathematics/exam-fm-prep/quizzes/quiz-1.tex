\noindent\textbf{Question 1.}  

A three-year bond pays annual coupons of \$50 and is redeemable at \$1000 at maturity. The bond yields an effective annual interest rate of 8\%.

Find the modified and Macaulay duration of the bond. \textit{Round your final answer to two decimals.}

\noindent\textbf{Question 3.}  

An investor has liabilities of $\$2{,}000$ due at time $t=1$, $\$2{,}000$ due at time $t=2$, and $\$4{,}000$ due at time $t=3$.  

To exactly match these liabilities, the investor considers purchasing the following three bonds:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item[(i)] A one-year par value bond with a coupon rate of 3\% and an effective annual interest rate of 6\%.
    
    \item[(ii)] A two-year zero-coupon bond with an effective annual interest rate of 7\%.
    
    \item[(iii)] A three-year par value bond with a coupon rate of 4\% and an effective annual interest rate of 8\%.
\end{itemize}

Calculate the total purchase price of the three bonds required to exactly match the liabilities. \textit{Round your final answer to two decimals.}
