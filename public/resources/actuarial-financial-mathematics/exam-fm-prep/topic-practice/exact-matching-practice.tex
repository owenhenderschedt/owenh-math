\begin{center}
    {\Large \textbf{Exact Matching Problem.}}
\end{center}

A construction firm is facing three liabilities of \$1{,}000, due at the end of each of the years $1$, $2$, and $3$.
There are three bonds available to match these liabilities, as follows:

\vspace{0.5em}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{>{\bfseries}p{2.2cm} p{12cm}}
\toprule
Bond I & A bond due at the end of year $1$ with a coupon rate of $1\%$ per year, valued at an effective annual yield rate of $14\%$.\\
\midrule
Bond II & A bond due at the end of year $2$ with a coupon rate of $2\%$ per year, valued at an effective annual yield rate of $15\%$.\\
\midrule
Bond III & A zero-coupon bond due at the end of year $3$ valued at an effective annual yield rate of $18\%$.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.5em}

Calculate the \underline{\textbf{total purchase price}} of the three bonds required to exactly match the liabilities.
