\textbf{Problems:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Question (1) on page 188 of textbook under section 3.8.

\item Mike purchases a perpetuity providing a series of increasing payments. The first
payment of 1200 is paid in a year and each payment is 2\% larger than the preceding
payment. Find the present value of the perpetuity given the annual effective interest rate
of 6\%.

\item Chris makes annual deposits into a bank account at the beginning of each year for 20
years. Chris's initial deposit is equal to 100, with each subsequent deposit $k$\% greater
than the previous year's deposit. The bank credits interest at an annual effective rate
of 5\%. At the end of 20 years, the accumulated value in Chris' account is equal to
7276.35. Given $k>5$, set up the equation of $k$. Use a graphing device to estimate the value of $k$.

\item Find the present value of an annuity-due with 17 annual payments of 100, 96, 92, \ldots, 36
with the annual effective interest rate of 5\%.

\item Olga buys a 5-year increasing annuity for $X$. Olga will receive 2 at the end of the first
month, 4 at the end of the second month, and for each month thereafter the payment
increases by 2. The nominal interest rate is 6\% convertible monthly. Calculate $X$.

\item Question (3) on page 189 of textbook under section 3.9.

\item Olga buys a 5-year increasing annuity for $X$. Olga will receive 2 at the end of the first
month, 4 at the end of the second month, and for each month thereafter the payment
increases by 2. The nominal interest rate is 6\% convertible monthly for the first two
years and 4.8\% convertible monthly for the last three years. Calculate $X$.
\end{enumerate}
